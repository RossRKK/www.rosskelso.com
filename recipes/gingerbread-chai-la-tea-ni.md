```recipe
"serve in a chilled coupe"(
    "shake over ice"(
        40ml "gingerbread chai vodka",
        30ml "1:1 honey syrup",
        30ml milk,
        "a squeeze of cinnamon syrup (optional)",
    ),
)
```

**Method**

1. Shake over ice.
2. Serve in a chilled coupe.

**Gingerbread chai vodka**

Use 1 tsp gingerbread chai per ~60ml vodka, steeped for a few hours (at least
10 mins). To make a batch:

```new-recipe
"strain through a coffee filter"(
    "steep a couple of days"(
        700ml "vodka (Brita-filter it if it's cheap)",
        6 tsp "gingerbread chai",
    ),
)
```

**Notes**

- Running cheap vodka through a Brita filter takes away the jet fuel flavour.
- A bigger serve: 60ml vodka, 45ml honey syrup, 45ml milk.
- Use smoked rum for an incredibly smoky version.

Inspired by the Bird & Blend gingerbread chai latte.
