```recipe
"double strain into a chilled coupe"(
    "shake"(
        30ml vodka,
        25ml "coffee liqueur, preferably Kahlúa",
        40ml "chilled espresso",
        20ml "caramel syrup",
        ice,
    ),
)
```

**Method**

1. Shake over ice.
2. Double strain into a chilled coupe.

Based on Nick Hamilton's recipe.
