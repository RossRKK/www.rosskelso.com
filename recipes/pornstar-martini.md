```recipe
"serve with"(
    "double strain into a chilled coupe"(
        "shake"(
            75ml "vanilla vodka",
            30ml Passoa,
            20ml "vanilla syrup",
            "pulp of 2 passion fruit",
            30ml "lime juice",
            ice,
        ),
    ),
    "a shot of Prosecco",
)
```

**Method**

1. Shake over ice.
2. Double strain into a chilled coupe.
3. Serve with a shot of Prosecco.

**Notes**

- 1 passion fruit is roughly 30ml of purée.

Based on the Cocktail Society recipe.
