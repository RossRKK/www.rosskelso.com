```recipe
"serve in a chilled coupe"(
    "shake over ice"(
        75ml "vanilla vodka",
        30ml Passoa,
        20ml "vanilla syrup",
        "pulp of 2 passion fruit",
        30ml "lime juice",
    ),
    "a shot of Prosecco",
)
```

**Method**

1. Shake over ice.
2. Serve in a chilled coupe with a shot of Prosecco.

**Notes**

- For one: 35ml vanilla vodka, 15ml Passoa, 10ml vanilla syrup, 1 passion
  fruit and 15ml lime juice.
- 1 passion fruit is roughly 30ml of purée.

Based on the Cocktail Society recipe.
