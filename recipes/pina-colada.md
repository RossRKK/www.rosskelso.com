```recipe
"strain into a highball"(
    "shake"(
        30ml "white rum",
        45ml "coconut cream",
        15ml "simple syrup",
        45ml "pineapple juice",
        15ml "lime juice",
        ice,
    ),
    ice,
)
```

**Method**

1. Shake over ice.
2. Strain into a highball over fresh ice.
