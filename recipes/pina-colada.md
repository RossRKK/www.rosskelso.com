```recipe
"dirty dump"(
    "shake over ice"(
        30ml "white rum",
        45ml "cream of coconut",
        45ml "pineapple juice",
        15ml "lime juice",
    ),
)
```

**Method**

1. Shake over ice.
2. Dirty dump: pour the whole lot, ice and all, straight into the glass
   without straining.

**Notes**

- No cream of coconut? Use 15ml simple syrup and 45ml coconut cream instead.
