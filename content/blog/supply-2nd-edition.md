# Supply

_2nd Edition_

This is a revised version of [Supply](@/blog/supply.md) that has been tweaked to
be simpler and more balanced than the original 2020 edition. You can play supply
here [https://supply.rosskelso.com](https://supply.rosskelso.com).

## What is Supply?

Supply is a deck building game where each player takes on the role of the rulers
of 2 rival kingdoms. You must manage the kingdom's economy, fortify its defences
and rally the troops if you hope to prevail.

## Setup

To play supply all you need is a standard 52 card deck of playing cards and some
way to track your kingdom's health (e.g. pen and paper).

Find the 2s, 3s and 4s of the 2 black suits, and the 2s and 3s of the red suits.

The player who most recently ruled a Kingdom goes first.

Deal the 2, 3 and 4 of spades, the 2 of diamonds and the 2 of hearts to the
player who will go first.

Deal the 2, 3 and 4 of clubs, the 3 of diamonds and the 3 of hearts to the
player who will go second.

Shuffle the remaining cards and place them on the table between you. Draw the
first 5 cards from the deck to form the _Supply row_.

Leave space for each player to have a deck to draw from (each player's deck is
initially empty), an area to play cards during their turn and a discard pile.

You must also leave space for the _scrap heap_ which is a face up pile of cards
which have been _scrapped_.

Each player begins the game with 30 health.

## How to play

On your turn you may play up to 3 cards from your hand. As you play each card
you resolve its effect.

At the end of your turn the cards you played (except barriers) are moved to your
discard pile (cards you haven't played remain in your hand). Then you draw cards
from your deck until you have 5 cards in your hand. If you cannot draw a card
from your deck you shuffle your discard pile to form a new deck. If you run out
of cards in your deck and discard pile you stop drawing.

You win the game if you reduce your opponent to zero health.

## Card abilities

Each card has an effect according to its suit. The strength of this effect is
determined by the number on the card. Face cards and aces contribute 10 points
to their suit effect and have an additional special ability.

### Spades ♠/Clubs ♣

_The spades and clubs are your kingdom's treasury. You cannot plan a war on
armies alone._

The spades and clubs are used to acquire new cards. When you play these cards
they contribute their value to your _money pool_.

Before the end of your turn you can spend money from your money pool to acquire
new cards from the supply row. When you acquire a card it is placed into your
discard pile. Each card costs its value except face cards and aces which cost 15
money.

Any unused money in your _money pool_ at the end of your turn is lost.

#### The Supply

Any time a card is acquired from the _supply row_ it is immediately replaced by
drawing a new card from the _supply deck_.

Once per turn you may _scrap_ all cards in the _supply row_ and place them in
the _scrap heap_. Replace the _supply row_ with new cards drawn from the _supply
deck_. If at any time you would need to draw a card from the supply deck but it
is empty, shuffle the _scrap heap_ to form a new supply deck.

### Diamonds ♦

_The diamonds are your kingdom's armies. Without them you cannot hope to defeat
your enemies._

The diamond cards are used to attack your opponent. Each diamond card
contributes its value to your _attack pool_.

If your opponent has no barriers you may spend attack from your _attack pool_ to
reduce their health.

If your opponent has barriers in play you may only use your attack to destroy a
barrier of your choice. To destroy a barrier you must spend attack from your
_attack pool_ matching the strength of the barrier. You cannot partially destroy
a barrier, you must beat its entire strength at once.

When a barrier is destroyed the cards composing it are returned to their owner's
discard pile.

Once you have destroyed your opponent's barriers you may use the remaining
attack in your _attack pool_ to attack their health as normal.

Any unused attack in your _attack pool_ at the end of your turn is lost.

### Hearts ♥

_The hearts are your kingdom's defences. Neglect their care and your kingdom
will surely fall._

The heart cards are used to defend your kingdom from your opponent's attacks.

All heart cards you play on the same turn form a barrier with strength equal to
the sum of the cards that compose it. These cards stay in play until they are
destroyed by an enemy attack. Once a barrier is in play it cannot be improved,
hearts played on a subsequent turn form a separate barrier.

Once a barrier is destroyed the cards composing it are returned to your discard
pile.

### Special Cards

Each of the face cards and the aces have a special ability in addition to their
suit ability.

#### Ace

When you play an ace you _may_ choose to _scrap_ a card from your hand. Move an
unplayed card from your hand to the scrap heap to remove it from your deck.

_It pays to run a tight ship_

#### Jack

When you play a Jack your opponent reveals their hand to you. Choose a card from
their hand which they must then discard to their discard pile immediately. They
will have 1 less card in hand for their next turn.

_The Jack has a spy in the enemy court_

#### Queen

When you play a queen draw another card into your hand. You may also play an
extra card this turn.

_The Queen is a deft hand at organising the troops_

#### King

When you play a king gain 5 health points.

_The King fosters the health of the Kingdom_

## Alternate rules

### Recession

In the recession format at the start of the game the kingdom is in a financial
crisis. Remove the 4s of clubs and spades from the starting hands.
