Nerve is a board game I originally designed in 2020. It is a multiplayer
variant of the [Royal Game of Ur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Game_of_Ur). You can
[download the board as an A3 PDF](/files/games/nerve-board-a3.pdf) to print.

## Rules

### Goal

The goal of Nerve is to get all of your pieces to the end first.

### Setup

Each player begins with 7 pieces of their colour next to their starting
zone (the end with 4 spaces).

### Gameplay

Players take turns clockwise, beginning with the player who was most
recently nervous.

On your turn roll 4 binary dice. A binary dice shows
either a 0 or a 1. Move any of your pieces a number of spaces equal to the
sum of the dice.

You follow a path starting from your coloured start zone, through the shared
zone and into your coloured end zone.

{{ inline_svg(path="img/nerve-route.svg", class="nerve-board", alt="Red’s route around the board, numbered 1 to 14: the four red start squares, the left octagon, the six shared squares, the right octagon and the two red end squares, then off the board. The other colours’ arms are faded.") }}

You may not move 2 of your pieces into the same square.

You must roll exactly in order to "bear off".

If you have no legal move you lose your turn.

If you land on another player's piece that piece is returned to the
beginning.

If you land on an arrow you may roll again.

### Differences to other race games

You do not have to roll a specific number to begin. If you have a piece at
the beginning you may simply move it into your start zone (if the appropriate
space is available).

Unlike the rosettes in the Royal Game of Ur, arrows are not safe spaces.
