The classic Kelso brothers' pancake recipe.

<!-- more -->

**Ingredients**

- 1 egg
- 100g plain flour
- 300ml milk

**Method**

1. Mix the ingredients together in a large mixing bowl.

2. Fry a small ladelful of pancake batter in a frying pan with butter
