**Ingredients**

- 500g potatoes
- 25g butter, at room temperature
- 125g plain flour, plus extra for rolling
- Salt and pepper

**Method**

1. Peel and boil the potatoes until tender. Cut them to be similar sizes.
2. Drain and leave them to air dry for a few minutes.
3. Mash the potatoes.
4. Mix the butter into the mashed potato.
5. Add the flour a bit at a time until it forms a stiff dough. You may not need
   all of it.
6. Turn out onto a floured surface and separate into 3 balls.
7. Pat each ball out into a flat circle, turning and lightly flouring both sides
   so it doesn't stick.
8. Prick all over with a fork.
9. Heat a large flat-bottomed pan until hot, then turn down to medium-low. Do
   not grease it; the butter in the dough will burn, and a light dusting of
   flour is enough to stop it sticking.
10. Fry for 3–4 minutes a side until browned, flipping more than once if needed.
11. Once each has been fried cut into quarters.
12. Return them to the pan with butter and fry them off.
