**Ingredients**

- 500g potatoes
- 25g butter, at room temperature
- 125g plain flour, plus extra for rolling
- Salt and pepper

**Method**

1. Peel and boil the potatoes until tender. Use potatoes of a similar size, or
   cut them down to match the smallest.
2. Drain and leave them to air dry for a few minutes.
3. Mash the butter into the potatoes, or rice the potatoes and mix the butter in
   after.
4. Add the flour a few tablespoons at a time until it forms a stiff dough. You
   may not need all of it.
5. Turn out onto a floured surface and separate into 3 balls. If the potato is
   still warm, let it cool a little before rolling.
6. Pat each ball out into a flat circle, turning and lightly flouring both sides
   so it doesn't stick. Cut the edges true with a small plate.
7. Prick all over with a fork, then cut into quarters — or score without cutting
   through and fry as one round, cutting after.
8. Heat a large flat-bottomed pan until hot, then turn down to medium-low. Do
   not grease it; the butter in the dough will burn, and a light dusting of
   flour is enough to stop it sticking.
9. Fry for 3–4 minutes a side, flipping more than once if needed. If it browns
   too fast, drop the heat.
10. Cool before eating, with butter and jam — or return them to the pan with
    butter and fry them off. They keep in an airtight container to fry or toast
    later, and they freeze.

**Notes from the test batch**

- Rolled thickness. _Thinner crisps up faster; thicker stays fluffy._
