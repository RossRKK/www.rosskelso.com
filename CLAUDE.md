# www.rosskelso.com

## Blog content is written by Ross, not AI

The prose on this site — posts, game rules, essays, recipes, guides — is
Ross's own writing. Don't draft it or rewrite it.

- Fine: typo and grammar fixes he asks for, pointing out gaps or ambiguities
  in his text, alt text, tags, links and citations, front matter, layout,
  diagrams, CSS, templates and build tooling.
- Not fine: writing new paragraphs, rules or arguments into a post, or
  rewording his prose beyond what he asked for. If something is missing,
  suggest it in chat (ideally quoting his own words back) and let him decide
  whether to add it, or leave a placeholder such as `<!-- TODO: rules -->`.
- Short factual lines that only describe a file (e.g. "You can download the
  board as an A3 PDF") are acceptable, but keep them minimal and say you added
  them so he can rewrite them.
